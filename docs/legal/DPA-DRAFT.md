## 0. Context for counsel

PAT / Patalign ("**Processor**") provides an accounting-firm ↔ vendor alignment
and benchmarking platform. Customers are **firms** (which are financial
institutions under the FTC Safeguards Rule and will flow vendor duties down to
us), **vendors**, and **consultants**. This DPA governs Processor's handling of
Customer Personal Data. It is intended to attach to the master agreement
(`[MSA reference]`).

## 1. Definitions

- **Controller / Customer** — the firm, vendor, or consultant organization.
- **Processor** — Patalign / `[legal entity]`.
- **Personal Data**, **Processing**, **Data Subject**, **Sub-processor** — as
  defined in `[applicable law: e.g. GDPR / CCPA / state privacy acts]`.
- **Customer Data** — account, organization, role, membership, assessment,
  product, and insight records submitted to or generated by the platform.

## 2. Scope and roles

2.1 Processor processes Customer Data **only on documented instructions** from
the Controller, including the instructions embodied in the product and this DPA.
2.2 Subject matter: provision of the PAT platform. Duration: term of the MSA plus
the retention windows in §7. Nature/purpose: `[hosting, scoring, benchmarking,
support]`. Categories of Data Subjects: `[firm/vendor/consultant staff]`.
Categories of Personal Data: name, work email, role, auth-provider identifiers,
session metadata, assessment responses. **No special-category data is solicited.**

## 3. Confidentiality & personnel

3.1 Processor ensures personnel authorized to process Customer Data are bound by
confidentiality. 3.2 Access is least-privilege; operator/review access is gated
and logged (see the operator audit record).

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1 Customer authorizes the sub-processors listed at `/security` (currently:
Vercel — hosting; Neon — database; Stripe — payments; Anthropic — optional AI
features). 5.2 Processor imposes data-protection terms on each sub-processor no
less protective than this DPA. 5.3 Processor gives `[N days]` notice of new
sub-processors and a right to object. 5.4 **No Customer Data is used to train
third-party models.**

## 6. Data-subject requests & assistance

6.1 Processor assists the Controller in responding to data-subject requests
(access, correction, deletion, portability) taking into account the nature of
processing. 6.2 The platform provides **tenant export** and **tenant deletion**
tooling (`scripts/ops/export-tenant.ts`, `scripts/ops/delete-tenant.ts`) to
support these obligations. 6.3 Processor notifies the Controller of a received
data-subject request rather than responding directly, unless instructed.

retained for `[7]` years as legal records; deletion receipts are retained
indefinitely as tombstones. 7.2 On termination, Processor returns and/or deletes
Customer Data within `[N days]`, subject to legal-hold and financial-record
retention, and provides a deletion receipt. 7.3 `[Attorney: reconcile retention
