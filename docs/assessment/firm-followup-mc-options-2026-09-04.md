Firm Alignment Assessment - All Questions

Every firm module asks the same 25 questions: 20 scored questions (0-5),
then 5 written follow-ups. The 20 scored questions are identical in
every module - "this area" means whichever module you are in. The 5
follow-ups are different in each module, but they are the same for every
firm - they do NOT change based on how the firm scored. That makes
converting them to multiple choice a clean content job.

The typing hint under each follow-up shows what the question is fishing
for - build the answer options from those.

The 20 scored questions (0-5 scale, same in every module)

1\. How clearly is the current-state approach defined in this area?

2\. How consistently is this area executed across the firm?

3\. How visible is current performance in this area to leadership?

4\. How disciplined are review and escalation practices in this area?

5\. How well do cross-functional handoffs work in this area?

6\. How manageable is day-to-day operational friction in this area?

7\. How ready is this area for repeatable automation support?

8\. How ready is this area for responsible AI-assisted work?

9\. How strong is data reliability for this area today?

10\. How well do current systems connect around this area?

11\. How much confidence do teams have in current controls here?

12\. How resilient is this area under deadline or volume pressure?

13\. How strong is change adoption in this area?

14\. How clearly are ownership and accountability defined here?

15\. How strong is vendor or tool oversight for this area?

16\. How clearly is measurable value understood here?

17\. How well is risk surfaced before it becomes operational drag?

18\. How aligned is this area with current firm strategy?

19\. How well can this area adapt to market or client change?

20\. How ready is this area for the next stage of PAT insight depth?

Notes for the multiple-choice conversion

Build 4-6 options per question from the hints, plus a final "Something
else" option with a required write-in box.

Every question also needs a "not a significant issue here" option, so
firms are never forced to invent a problem.

Keep single-select on any question that says "biggest" or "most" - the
point of those words is to force one answer.

A few questions mention PAT by name - keep or neutralize as you prefer
while rewording.

Plain language throughout, same as your question-bank edits.
